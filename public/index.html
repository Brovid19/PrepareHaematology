    Install Prepare Haematology

    Add the app to your home screen or desktop. It opens in its own window, works offline, and keeps your flashcard progress, answers and settings on this device. No app store or download needed.

    Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet)

    1. Tap Install App above, or open the browser menu (⋮).
    2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
    3. Open PrepareHaem from your home screen. Long-press the icon for shortcuts to Review, Practice and Mock.

    iPhone and iPad (Safari)

    1. Open this site in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
    3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

    1. Click Install App above, or the install icon at the right of the address bar.
    2. The app opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu, Dock or Launchpad.
