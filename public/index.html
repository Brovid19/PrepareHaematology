Collapsible Approved Guideline Digests

Implementation and value of providing a multidisciplinary haematology and gynaecology service
Biss et al. • Br J Haematol 2026 • General Haematology / Coagulation • Evidence Grade 1B
▼

Clinical Focus: Heavy Menstrual Bleeding (HMB) in women with inherited bleeding disorders, thrombosis risk during hormonal contraception/HRT, and anticoagulation management during pregnancy.

  • Joint haematology-gynaecology clinics significantly reduce time to definitive haemostatic diagnosis for women with menorrhagia.
  • First-line medical therapy for HMB in von Willebrand Disease (VWD) includes tranexamic acid and levonorgestrel-releasing intrauterine systems (LNG-IUS).
  • Mandatory MDT review prior to uterine artery embolization or hysterectomy in severe factor deficiencies.

Point of care testing in general haematology: An update to the British Society for Haematology
Tam et al. • Br J Haematol 2026 • Laboratory & Quality Assurance • Evidence Grade 1A
▼

Clinical Focus: Quality governance, ISO 22870 accreditation standards, internal quality control (IQC), and external quality assessment (EQA) for handheld coagulometers, haemoglobinometers, and platelet function analysers.

  • All POCT haematology devices must sit under hospital central laboratory clinical governance.
  • MicroINR / CoaguChek INR measurements are unreliable at INR > 4.5 or in the presence of antiphospholipid antibodies; venous laboratory confirmation is mandatory.
  • Rotational thromboelastometry (ROTEM) / TEG in trauma and postpartum haemorrhage must adhere to daily calibrated IQC runs.

Diagnosis and monitoring of thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura: Guidance for clinical laboratories
MacDonald et al. • Br J Haematol 2026 • Haemostasis & Thrombosis • Evidence Grade 1A
▼

Clinical Focus: Diagnostic criteria for immune and congenital TTP, rapid ADAMTS13 activity assays (FRETS-VWF73, chemiluminescent immunoassays), antibody quantification, and pre-plasma exchange sampling.

  • Pre-treatment citrate sample for ADAMTS13 activity must be obtained prior to initiating therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE) or caplacizumab.
  • Severe deficiency defined as ADAMTS13 activity < 10% (< 0.10 IU/mL).
  • Clinical scores (PLASMIC score ≥ 6 or French Score 2) justify immediate prompt start of caplacizumab, corticosteroids, and plasma exchange while awaiting definitive enzyme results.

The diagnosis and management of primary mediastinal B-cell lymphoma: A British Society for Haematology guideline
Osborne et al. • Br J Haematol 2026 • Malignant Haematology • Evidence Grade 1B
▼

Clinical Focus: Distinguishing PMBCL from Classical Hodgkin Lymphoma and DLBCL, mediastinal core biopsy immunohistochemistry (CD20+, CD30+ weak/heterogeneous, CD15-, MAL+, TRAF1+), DA-EPOCH-R vs R-CHOP-14, and PET-CT Deauville scoring to spare consolidative radiotherapy.

  • DA-EPOCH-R is the preferred induction regimen to achieve high cure rates and avoid mediastinal radiotherapy in young patients.
  • End-of-treatment PET-CT with Deauville score 1–3 justifies complete observation without radiation.
  • Relapsed/refractory PMBCL responds strongly to checkpoint blockade (Pembrolizumab / Nivolumab) and CD19 CAR-T cell therapy due to 9p24.1 (PD-L1/PD-L2) copy gain.

Single Best Answer (SBA) questions with strict BSH guideline evidence rationales.

Question 1 • Haemostasis BSH TTP 2026

A 34-year-old woman presents with severe microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, thrombocytopenia (platelets 14 × 10⁹/L), and mild fluctuating confusion. Plasma creatinine is 110 µmol/L. Coagulation screen (PT, APTT, fibrinogen) is normal. Which of the following is the single most critical diagnostic action before initiating therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE)?

A. Take a whole blood sample for bone marrow trephine biopsy
B. Obtain a pre-treatment citrate plasma sample for ADAMTS13 activity and antibody assay
C. Administer 1 unit of adult platelets to prevent intracranial hemorrhage
D. Perform urgent renal biopsy to exclude atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome (aHUS)
Correct Answer: B
Evidence Grounding: According to the 2026 British Society for Haematology TTP Guidance (MacDonald et al.), obtaining a baseline pre-transfusion/pre-TPE citrated plasma sample is mandatory because donor plasma will immediately replenish circulating ADAMTS13 activity and mask the diagnostic threshold (<10%). Platelet transfusions are contraindicated in TTP except in life-threatening bleeding.
Question 2 • Malignant Haematology BSH PMBCL 2026

A 28-year-old female presents with a 9 cm anterior mediastinal mass causing superior vena cava compression. Core biopsy confirms Primary Mediastinal B-cell Lymphoma (PMBCL). She completes 6 cycles of DA-EPOCH-R. An end-of-treatment FDG PET-CT shows a residual mass with Deauville Score 2. What is the recommended management according to the 2026 BSH guidelines?

A. Consolidative mediastinal radiotherapy (30–36 Gy)
B. High-dose therapy and autologous stem cell transplantation (ASCT)
C. Clinical observation and surveillance without radiotherapy
D. Immediate salvage CD19 CAR-T cell infusion
Correct Answer: C
Evidence Grounding: The 2026 BSH PMBCL Guideline (Osborne et al.) recommends that patients treated with DA-EPOCH-R who achieve complete metabolic response (Deauville Score 1–3) on end-of-treatment PET-CT should be observed without mediastinal radiotherapy. Radiotherapy is reserved for Deauville 4–5 residual metabolic activity.
